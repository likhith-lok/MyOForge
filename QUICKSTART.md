# MyoForge - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Get Started in 30 Seconds

### 1. **Open the App**
Simply open `index.html` in your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)

### 2. **Sign In with Gmail**
Click "Sign in with Gmail" button (simulated for demo)

### 3. **Choose Your Start**
- Jump straight to your garage
- Take the optional enthusiast tour

### 4. **Start Building**
- Create a new car with a custom name
- Select a category (Engine, Suspension, Body, etc.)
- Browse parts and hover to see details
- Click "Add Part" to install on your car
- Watch your build update in real-time

## 🎮 Basic Controls

### Landing Page
- Sign in to enter the enthusiast workshop

### Onboarding
- Choose "I Got This" to continue directly
- Choose "Show Me Around" for the optional tour
- Skip the tour to enter the garage

### Car Builder
- **Click category tabs** to switch between sections
- **Hover on parts** to reveal description and significance
- **Click "Add Part"** to add to your car
- **Click "Remove"** on preview panel to remove parts
- **Click "Save Car"** to persist your build

### Car Garage
- **Click "New Car Build"** to create a car
- **Click "Edit"** on a car to customize it
- **Click "Delete"** to remove a car

## 💾 Your Data

All your cars and customizations are saved in your browser's localStorage. This means:
- ✅ Your data persists when you close the browser
- ✅ Your data is private and local to your device
- ✅ No data is sent to any server (demo version)
- ✅ You can export your builds for sharing (future feature)

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### "My data disappeared!"
- Check browser storage settings
- Clear cache might erase localStorage
- Data is stored locally per browser/device

### "Animations look choppy"
- Try closing other browser tabs
- Update your browser
- GPU acceleration helps performance

### "Can't add parts"
- Make sure you've created a car first
- Click "Add Part" button on part tile back
